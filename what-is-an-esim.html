eSIM basics

What Is an eSIM and How Does It Work for Travel?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into a compatible phone, tablet, or connected device. Instead of inserting a plastic SIM card, you install a mobile data profile with a QR code or activation details.

Why travelers choose eSIM plans

Travel eSIMs are popular because they can be purchased before departure and activated quickly after arrival. You keep your regular SIM for calls or messages while using a separate data plan for maps, ride apps, messaging, email, and browsing.

How eSIM activation works

After buying a plan, you receive an eSIM profile. On most phones, you open cellular settings, choose to add an eSIM, scan the QR code, and follow the prompts. Once installed, you select the eSIM for mobile data and turn on data roaming if the plan instructions require it.

eSIM vs. physical SIM

A physical SIM can be lost, damaged, or hard to buy after landing. An eSIM keeps the setup digital, which helps when you arrive late, cross borders, or want connectivity before finding a local store.

Before you buy

Check that your phone supports eSIM and is not carrier locked. Then choose a data bundle based on trip length, destination, and how much you expect to use maps, video, hotspots, and social apps.
